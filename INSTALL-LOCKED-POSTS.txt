Step 6, locked posts: starscapes-locked-posts-19oct.zip   (and ONE SQL file to run in Supabase)

WHAT THIS DOES
A post can now belong to a pass. When it does, its WORDS are kept in a separate protected table (post_bodies), and the DATABASE decides who may read them (so it cannot be got round from the website):
   * YOU (the owner) can always read everything;
   * anyone who HOLDS that post's pass can read it;
   * anyone at all can read the FIRST THREE posts of each pass (the three OLDEST published posts with that pass);
   * everyone else gets nothing back, and the post's page shows a gate instead: "This is part of the Blueprints pass. The first three posts in every pass are free to read. This isn't one of them,
     so it opens with the Blueprints pass." with links to log in and choose passes (or, while memberships are closed, "Passes aren't open yet").
The title, picture, date and room of a locked post stay public (that is what makes people want the pass). Only the words are protected.

NOTHING CHANGES UNTIL YOU CHOOSE A PASS ON A POST. Every post stays public as it is.

HOW YOU USE IT
 * New post and Edit post each have a new choice called "Pass". "Free for everyone" is an ordinary post. Choose a pass to keep the words of that post for people who hold it.
 * Choosing a pass on an existing post and saving moves its words into the protected store FIRST (so they can never be lost), then leaves the post itself without words.
 * Changing a post back to "Free for everyone" puts the words back into the post and clears the protected copy.
 * Until the SQL has been run, the Pass choice does not show at all, and everything works as before. So installing the code first, then the SQL, is safe.

THREE THINGS I DECIDED FOR YOU (tell me if you want any different)
 1. A membership whose payment has FAILED (Stripe is still retrying) keeps its passes open. They close if the membership becomes unpaid or ends. A membership you have cancelled keeps its passes until the end of what was paid for.
 2. "The first three" means the three OLDEST published posts of that pass (so Blueprints 1, 2 and 3 stay free if they are your oldest Blueprints). Posts are counted per pass.
 3. The gate (the box that replaces the words) is not in your Figma designs: it is my own design. Please look at it and tell me what you would change.

THE ONE SQL STEP (in Supabase: SQL editor, clear it, PASTE THE SQL ITSELF, not the file name, make sure nothing is highlighted, press Run; it says "Success. No rows returned."; safe to run twice)

   To check it (it should list 4 policies):

HOW TO TRY IT
 Because YOU are the owner, you can always read everything, so you cannot see the gate while signed in as yourself. To see it:
 1. Make FOUR small test posts with the pass "Book Club" (New post, Pass: Book Club). The first three are free to read; the FOURTH is locked.
 2. Open the fourth one in a PRIVATE window (not signed in): you should see the gate, and none of its words. Open the first one the same way: you should see its words.
 3. As yourself, all four show their words.
 4. In Supabase's Table Editor, look at the posts table: a post with a pass has an EMPTY content, and its words are in post_bodies.
 5. To see a pass holder get in, you would need a second test account that has bought a Book Club pass in test mode (optional; tell me if you want to do that).
 6. Delete the test posts when you are done (deleting a post removes its protected words too).

TO LOCK AN EXISTING POST (for example your Blueprints posts): Edit post, Pass: Blueprints, Save. Remember the three oldest Blueprints posts stay free to read.

IF YOU WANT IT ALL BACK: first put any locked posts back to "Free for everyone" with Edit post; then the undo lines at the top of sql/locked-posts.sql; then git revert HEAD and git push.
