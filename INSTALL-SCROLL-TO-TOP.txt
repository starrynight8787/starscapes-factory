PAGES OPEN AT THE TOP: FIX FOR "THE NEXT PAGE OPENS SCROLLED DOWN"   starscapes-scroll-to-top-22oct.zip
(Code and tests only. No Supabase step. Released through a preview first.)

THE PROBLEM (found by your tester, 22 October 2026): scroll down a page, click a link in the footer, and the next page opens already scrolled down.
THE CAUSE: the site is a single-page app (React Router's BrowserRouter), so clicking a link swaps the page's content without the browser ever loading a new page, and the browser keeps your scroll position. Nothing in the site reset it (only a few buttons in the admin pages did their own scrolling). This affects EVERY link on the site, not just the footer.

THE FIX: a small helper, src/components/ScrollToTop.jsx, mounted in src/App.jsx right beside RouteTitle (the helper that already runs on every page change). It puts you at the top of every page you reach by clicking a link.
IT DELIBERATELY LEAVES ALONE:
 - the first time the site opens (a reload keeps the browser's own place);
 - Back and Forward (the browser puts you back where you were);
 - a change that only alters the ?query (your shelf genre filters and searches, which would otherwise jump to the top each time you type);
 - a link to a #section, which now scrolls to that section instead (the skip link and "Join the conversation" keep working as they did);
 - the shelf page's own smooth scroll to a genre (it runs after the books load, as it did).

TESTS: 12 new in test/scroll-to-top.test.jsx (nothing happens when the site first opens; a link opens the next page at the top; it does so for every page; Back and Forward do not move you; a ?query-only change does not jump, including straight after pressing Back; a #section link brings that section into view; a link to a section that is not there goes to the top; a badly written link cannot break the page; App mounts it exactly once, before the pages; the site is still wrapped in a router). 9 deliberate breakages: 8 caught, and the 9th changes nothing you could see (the first page is protected twice over), so it cannot be caught.
WHOLE SUITE on a copy of your newest commit (50e8713) with this installed: 3,765 tests, 0 failing (that includes this installer's own 9 safety tests); build fine; 12 functions; health check 216 of 216.

NOT CHANGED, FOR YOU TO KNOW:
 - Back and Forward rely on the browser to put you back where you were. On a page that loads its content late, it can land slightly off. That is how it behaved before, and fixing it properly is a larger job.
 - For keyboard and screen-reader users, the keyboard focus stays where it was after a link opens a new page. Moving it to the top of the new page (the main content) is a worthwhile follow-up for the accessibility statement. Say if you would like it.
