WHAT CHANGES, AND WHAT DOES NOT
  * On a computer-sized screen (900 pixels wide or more) the sign-up box in the footer now stretches across the whole footer, in two columns: the title and the two short paragraphs on the left, the form on the right.

  * PHONES AND TABLETS ARE EXACTLY AS BEFORE (one column, 350 wide and 466 tall on a phone).
  * The words are the same. The ORDER is the same: email address, then the tick box, then the Sign up button, so a keyboard user is taken round it in the same order as what they see. Nothing is reordered with CSS, and a test fails if anyone does.
  * The box still shows only when sign-ups are open (NEWSLETTER_OPEN is "yes"), and it is still hidden on the Sarah Junior's Clubhouse pages.
