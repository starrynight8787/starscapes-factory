(Code, wording and tests. No Supabase step. ONE SETTING IN STRIPE comes first. Released through a preview first.)

WHY: the law wants the customer to be given the cancellation information, who pays to send a print back, and the model cancellation form BEFORE they pay. If it is missing, the 14-day cancellation period can stretch to up to 12 months.

WHAT IT DOES (what you will SEE)
 1. ON THE CART PAGE, above the pay button: a "Before you pay" box with four plain lines (you can cancel any time until 14 days after your print is delivered; the large framed prints sentence: we arrange and pay for those returns, you pay for the rest;
    a faulty print is put right and you do not pay to send it back; pressing Pay is an offer, and the contract begins when we email to confirm), and links to the Terms and the Returns and refunds page (which has the cancellation form), opening in a new tab.
 2. ON STRIPE'S PAGE (where the contract is really made): a REQUIRED tick box ("I have read and agree to the Terms and conditions and the Returns and refunds page, which includes my 14-day right to cancel"), and the same returns summary beside the pay button.
    The customer cannot pay without ticking. Stripe records each acceptance on the order, which is better proof than anything we could store ourselves.
 3. A NEW RAIL: REAL payments stay closed until the legal pages are published (they are drafts until SFW-186), because the tick box points at them. Test payments are NOT held back, so you can try checkout now. When the pages are published (SFW-186), set
    LEGAL_PAGES_PUBLISHED to true in src/cart/beforeYouPay.js in the SAME change that sets ready: true on the legal pages: a test fails if the two disagree.
 4. If Stripe refuses because the Terms address is missing in its settings, the checkout says so plainly ("the address of the Terms page still has to be entered in Stripe") instead of a vague "try again".
ALL THE WORDING COMES FROM ONE FILE (src/cart/beforeYouPay.js), used by the cart box AND Stripe's page, and the large framed prints sentence is the one used on the Returns page and in the order email (src/data/returns.js), so they cannot disagree.

*** FIRST, ONE SETTING IN STRIPE (before you try checkout, or Stripe will refuse to show the tick box) ***
  Stripe dashboard > Settings > Business > Public details (the label may be slightly different) > the "Terms of service" address:   https://starscapesfactory.com/page/terms
  Save it. If Stripe shows this setting separately for test mode and live mode, set it in BOTH. If you cannot find it, press Pay on the preview: the message will tell you if it is missing, and then tell me what you see in Stripe.
  The page at that address is still a draft until SFW-186, so a visitor could not read it yet: that is why real payments stay closed.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO (on purpose)
  * It does not publish the legal pages (SFW-186), and it does not open real payments: the rail above, CHECKOUT_OPEN and ALLOW_LIVE_CHECKOUT all still apply.
  * It does not put a returns line on the print pages (the cart box and Stripe's page carry it); that can be added later if you want it.
  * It does not change the order emails.
