2. THE CHANGE RULE (a WORKING RULE: change it by changing one number, CHANGE_WINDOW_DAYS in src/membership/changeWindow.js, and the Terms sentence)
   A member can change their passes only in the 7 DAYS BEFORE THEIR NEXT PAYMENT. The change starts at once and the new price starts with that payment (nothing charged or refunded when you change). This closes the loophole where someone adds
   passes the day after paying and removes them just before the next payment.
   * The server refuses a change outside the window, says when it opens, and touches nothing.
   * The Change my passes page, outside the window, says: "You can change your passes in the 7 days before your next payment. Your next payment is on 19 November 2026, so you can change them from 12 November 2026."
   * If we do not have a next-payment date for a member, the window is OPEN (nobody is locked out because of a gap in our records).
   * Only changing is limited: the billing page and cancelling work at any time.
   * For a YEARLY member this means changes are possible only in the last week of the year. That is the price of the simple rule. If you would rather charge the difference when someone upgrades, tell me.
