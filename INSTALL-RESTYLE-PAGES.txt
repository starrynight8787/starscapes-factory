The older pages restyled: starscapes-restyle-pages-17oct.zip  (tasks SFW-200 and SFW-201, part 1)

WHAT IT CHANGES (the pages visitors see)
- The post list at /blog: the same cards as the room pages, a filter of the tags on your posts (with "All"), the whole row a link. Plain words if the
  posts cannot load (it no longer shows the database's own message).
- A single post: the room's name as a small label, the date in full ("15 October 2026"), the title in Schoolbell, the header picture (only if its
  address is safe), the words with their line breaks, and the tags. Edit and Delete show only to you, as before.
- The plain text pages (About, Contact and so on): the same page style, with a way back. (They still hold placeholder text: see the launch checklist.)
- The footer's Follow links now read from contact.js, the one place your addresses live, so YouTube, Instagram and TikTok appear. Threads stays hidden
  until it has an address.
- A post's tag links used to send people to the home page, which ignores tags. They now go to /blog filtered to that tag.

WHAT IT DOES NOT CHANGE YET (on purpose)
- New post, Edit post, New product (only you see them), log in and sign up, the old shop pages, and the old index.css rules. The old rules stay until you
  have looked at these pages, because taking them out is the step most likely to change how another page looks. Tell Claude when you are happy and
  those go in the next update.
- The footer's four-column design waits for the legal pages (it links to pages that are not published yet).

INSTALL (from the project folder, in this order)
1. unzip -o ~/Downloads/starscapes-restyle-pages-17oct.zip
   (this also replaces two test files, article-titles and foundations, with updated versions)
2. python3 tools/add-restyle-pages.py       (it checks all four files first; if anything is unexpected it changes nothing)
3. node tools/health-check.mjs               (all 91 checks should pass)
4. npm run build                             (must finish without errors)
5. git add .
   git status                                (no files ending .before- should be listed)
   git commit -m "SFW-200 Restyle the post list, single post and text pages; footer social links"
   git push
6. Wait for Vercel to say Ready.

THEN LOOK (this is the check that matters: Claude cannot see the pages)
Open each of these on your computer AND your phone, and send Claude a screenshot of anything that looks wrong:
   /blog
   /blog?tag=<one of your tags>
   one single post (click one from the list)
   /page/contact
   the footer at the bottom of any page (the Follow links)
Look for: text that is hard to read, anything cut off, a picture that is stretched, a button that looks blank or white, and anything that jumps when you click.
