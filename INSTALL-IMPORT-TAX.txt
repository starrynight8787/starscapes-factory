IMPORT TAXES: TELLING THE BUYER, BEFORE THEY PAY   starscapes-import-tax-21oct.zip   (SFW-16)
(Code, wording and one test. No Supabase step, nothing to set in Vercel. Nothing to change in Creative Hub: duty-paid delivery stays OFF.)

THE DECISION (Gill, 21 October 2026: "a for launch"): leave duty-paid delivery (DDP) off for Canada, and tell the buyer plainly, before they pay, that Canadian orders may carry import tax and a courier fee on delivery.

WHAT CREATIVE HUB'S OWN HELP PAGES SAY (read 21 October; the pages are 70 to 135 days old, so confirm in Creative Hub, Settings, Fulfilment): UK, EU and US orders carry NO customs charge, because each print is made inside that region (studios in the UK, Germany and the US). DDP exists for only seven countries (Canada, Australia, Norway, New Zealand, Switzerland, Iceland, Mexico), adds a flat courier handling fee per order plus the duty and tax (worked out from the production value and shipping, not your retail price), and needs "Upgrade to Courier" on. So of your six free-delivery countries, only CANADA (printed in the US, shipped across the border) can produce a customs bill.

WHAT CHANGES
 1. THE WORDING, ONCE (src/cart/launchCountries.js): "Delivering to Canada: your country's import tax and a courier handling fee may be charged when the parcel arrives. They are not included in our prices. Orders to the UK, Ireland, Germany, France and the US have none."
 2. WHERE THE BUYER SEES IT: as a fourth fact under Add to cart on every print page; in the cart, under the line about free delivery; and on Stripe's own address step, where the country is chosen (added to the message that already says delivery is free).
 3. THE TERMS ("Orders from outside the UK") now say each print is made in the region it is delivered to (UK, Germany for the EU, the US for the US), so there are no import taxes within those regions; Canada is the exception, and the buyer is responsible. It replaces the old line that suggested any order outside the UK could be charged. THE RETURNS page ("If you ordered from outside the UK") says the same, and that Canadian charges are paid to customs and the courier, so we cannot refund them. Both pages are still drafts, owner-only.
 4. A SAFETY CATCH: if a country is ever added to the free-delivery list that is NOT printed in its own region (for example Switzerland or Norway, which SFW-187 may add), a test fails until its import tax wording has been decided (and duty-paid delivery considered).

NOT CHANGED: the print pages' own delivery line ("Delivery cost is shown at checkout, which opens soon") is still waiting for your wording (SFW-9, then SFW-10).
