YouTube Series and Free Ebook: starscapes-ebook-youtube-17oct.zip  (task SFW-196, part 2)

WHAT IT ADDS (both behind their doors, which stay CLOSED until you open them in Admin, Open a door)
1. YOUTUBE SERIES (/sarah-jrs-den/youtube-series), from your Figma design. It fills itself from Sarah Jr.'s channel (@sarahjradventures) and shows the latest
   12 videos, newest first, with a Subscribe button. It leaves out YouTube Shorts (to include them, change INCLUDE_SHORTS to true in lib/youtubeFeed.js).
   PRIVACY: nothing from YouTube loads in a visitor's browser until they press play on a video. The little pictures are passed along by your own site, and the
   video plays in YouTube's privacy-friendly player. The list is remembered for 15 minutes, so YouTube is asked only a few times an hour.
2. FREE EBOOK (/leadership-lounge/free-ebook), from your two Figma designs: the sign-up page and the thank-you page. A visitor gives their email and ticks the
   box (the tick is their agreement to the emails, in the words from your design), and the site keeps the address, the words they agreed to, and when.
   While there is no PDF yet, the thank-you page says the ebook is being finished and does NOT offer a download or claim an email was sent.
   Emails come FROM imagine@starscapesfactory.com.

TWO THINGS THAT NEED YOU (neither stops anything else working)
 A. THE DATABASE FOR SIGN-UPS: run sql/ebook-signups.sql once in Supabase (see below). Until you do, the sign-up says "That did not go through just now".
 B. THE PDF, WHEN IT IS READY: save it in the project at public/downloads/ (make the folder downloads inside public), for example
    public/downloads/leading-from-the-middle.pdf. Then open src/ebook/ebookConfig.js, find   file: '',   and change it to
    file: '/downloads/leading-from-the-middle.pdf',   Push. The page then offers the download, and each new sign-up is emailed the link.
    (People who signed up before the PDF existed are in your Supabase table, so you can email them the link yourself.)

THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE REAL PEOPLE SIGN UP
 * The tick box promises "your free blog posts by email" with a way to unsubscribe. This site keeps the list but does NOT send the posts or run unsubscribes:
   when you start sending them, use an email tool that handles the unsubscribe link for you, and import the addresses from the Supabase table.
 * Your Privacy Policy must say you keep these emails and why. The one on the site today is still a placeholder (it is on the launch checklist, SFW-185).

NEEDS: the suggest and ledger update (starscapes-suggest-ledger-17oct.zip), which you have. The script stops and says so if it is not there.

INSTALL (from the project folder, in this order; ONLY run git add . at the very end)
1. unzip -o ~/Downloads/starscapes-ebook-youtube-17oct.zip
   (this also replaces three test files with updated versions)
2. python3 tools/add-ebook-youtube.py        (it checks both files first; if anything is unexpected it changes nothing)
3. node tools/health-check.mjs                (all 112 checks should pass)
4. npm run build                              (must finish without errors)
5. git add .
   git status                                 (no files ending .before- should be listed)
   git commit -m "SFW-196 YouTube Series and Free Ebook pages"
   git push
6. Wait for Vercel to say Ready.

THEN, IN SUPABASE (paste the LINES inside the file, never its name; this is in TERMINAL then SUPABASE, two different places)
   a. In Terminal, in the project folder:   pbcopy < sql/ebook-signups.sql      (it copies the contents and prints nothing)
   b. In Supabase's SQL editor: clear the box, press Cmd+V. You should see a file starting "-- FREE EBOOK SIGN-UPS".
   c. Press Run. It should say "Success. No rows returned."
   d. To check, run:   select count(*) from pg_policies where tablename = 'ebook_signups';    It should say 0 (that is right: no visitor can touch the table).

THEN LOOK (you can see both pages now as the owner, while their doors are closed)
   /sarah-jrs-den/youtube-series   : your videos should appear. Press play on one.
   /leadership-lounge/free-ebook   : try signing up with your own email. Then look in Supabase: Table Editor, ebook_signups, for your row.
