THE NEWSLETTER SAYS IT IS FREE   starscapes-newsletter-free-21oct.zip   (SFW-250)
(One sentence on the sign-up box, and tests that keep it true. No Supabase step, nothing to set in Vercel.)

THE ANSWER TO "CAN PEOPLE SIGN UP FOR FREE?"
 Yes, it always was free in practice: all someone gives is an email address and a tick. No account, no pass, no payment. I checked every part of it: the box, the page that talks to the server, and the server's public side (sign up, confirm, leave) never ask who someone is, never look for a pass, and never touch Stripe.
 What was missing was SAYING so. With passes on sale a few inches away, a visitor could reasonably wonder. Now the box reads:
    Newsletter
    Free, with no account or pass needed. New posts and news from Starscapes Factory, by email.
 (The words people tick are unchanged, because they are recorded as proof and match the Privacy policy.)
 Remember the box stays hidden until NEWSLETTER_OPEN is "yes" in Vercel, which waits for the Privacy policy.

TESTS (7 new; I broke the sign-up 7 ways on purpose and every one was caught)
 The box says free and "no account or pass needed"; it asks for nothing but an email and a tick (no name, password, card or pass); it works for a signed-out visitor who holds no pass; nothing in the box or the page that talks to the server mentions a sign-in, a session, a membership, a token or Stripe; the public server code never asks who someone is; the footer shows the box to everyone, with no condition; the words people agree to mention no price or pass.
 I also looked at it in a real browser (with the newsletter pretending to be open): it reads clearly on the dark footer card.
