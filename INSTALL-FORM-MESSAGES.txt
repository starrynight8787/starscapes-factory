2. A BUG FOUND ON THE WAY, AND FIXED FOR EVERY FORM. My first test of the new messages failed in an odd way: after a failed send, typing "A print" into the title left only "A". The cause is in the shared summary box (src/components/FormMessages.jsx), not in the new form. It moved the keyboard focus to itself whenever it was given a NEW list of problems, and most forms on the site build a new list every time they are drawn, which is every keystroke. So once a form had shown a problem, EVERY LETTER TYPED TO FIX IT pulled the person out of the field and back to the summary. That affects, among others, the free ebook and newsletter sign-up, the suggestion box, New post and Edit post, and the admin forms. It is easy to miss with a mouse and a quick test, and very disruptive for anyone using a keyboard or a screen reader.
   Now the box takes the focus only when (a) its problems are different from last time, or (b) a form on the page has just been sent (so a screen reader still hears "there is a problem" if the same problem comes back), and never just because the form was drawn again. A form elsewhere on the page, such as the footer's sign-up box, does not set it off.
   One older test in test/form-messages.test.jsx simulated "the form failed again" by merely drawing the summary again with an identical list: that is exactly the situation that caused the bug. It now simulates what really happens (the form is sent, then the same problems come back). Its meaning is unchanged.
