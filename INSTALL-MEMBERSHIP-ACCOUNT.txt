(My passes, Change my passes, cancelling and starting again, and the Subscribe button)

WHAT THIS ADDS
  * MY PASSES, at /membership/passes. For someone holding passes it shows how many, what they pay (with the launch offer and its end date, or a promo code's discount by name), when the next payment is,
    the card ending, and two lists: "Open now" and "Not open". Buttons: "Change my passes", and "Manage payment or cancel", which opens STRIPE'S OWN PAGE (change card, receipts, cancel).
    It also has the other views in your design: "My membership" for a free member ("Want to open more of the story?"), and the Cancelled view ("Your passes are cancelled. They stay open until 16 November 2026,
    and you will not be charged again.") with "Start again", which takes the cancellation back.
    TWO VIEWS ARE MINE, NOT DRAWN: "Ended" (passes that have run out, with a way to choose again) and "Payment needed" (a payment failed; it opens Stripe so they can fix their card).
  * CHANGE MY PASSES, at /membership/change: the passes they hold are ticked; tick or untick; it shows "From £7 a month to £10 a month" and says what is being added or removed; Save, or Cancel.
  * THE SUBSCRIBE BUTTON in the menu goes to the passes page once memberships are open (until then it still goes to ordinary sign-up, as now).
  * One quiet safety change: every message to Stripe now asks for one fixed version of Stripe's interface, so what Stripe sends back cannot change shape underneath us.

A DECISION FOR YOU: HOW A CHANGE IS CHARGED (I picked the simplest, and your design said something different)
  Your design said "Stripe works out any small adjustment for today, and shows it to you before you pay". Showing that adjustment first needs something I cannot test from here, so I built this instead, and
  I changed the words in your design to match:
     "The change starts today. Your new price applies from your next payment. Nothing is charged today, and nothing is refunded for a pass you take away."
  So adding a pass opens it straight away and costs more from the NEXT payment; removing one closes it straight away and costs less from the next payment. It is easy for people to understand, and it never
  surprises a card. The cost to you: someone could add a pass, use it, and remove it before the next payment without paying for it. If you would rather charge the difference today, tell me and I will change it.
  ALSO ADDED TO YOUR DESIGN: when someone ADDS a pass it opens straight away, so (as at checkout) they must tick "Open my passes now. I understand that once they open, I lose my 14-day right to cancel and get a refund."
  A tick box now sits above "Save my passes" on both Change my passes screens. Removing a pass needs no tick.
