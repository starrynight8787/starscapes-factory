Membership, step 4: starscapes-membership-payment-19oct.zip
(the payment in Stripe's TEST mode, the welcome page, and the fifth pass, Come Write With Me)

WHAT THIS ADDS
  * COME WRITE WITH ME is now a pass, in your words: "Let's write a story together. I write, you decide. Where the story goes will be decided by you." (I added only the
    apostrophe in "Let's" and a comma.) It sits with the Drafting Desk's other two. The page says "Your passes: 3 of 5". The price ladder is unchanged (four or more is £12), as you chose.
    The line under "Free, always" now also says the first three Come Write With Me sessions are free to join. Your Figma design has the fifth card on every screen.
  * THE PAYMENT. "Continue to payment" now takes a LOGGED-IN person to Stripe's own checkout page, for a subscription to the passes they ticked. It sends only the passes, monthly or yearly,
    the tick-box agreement and a promo code; the server works out the price itself, so no price can be changed from a browser.
      - The launch offer (half price for the first year) is applied automatically as a Stripe discount. A promo code from Stripe REPLACES it (Stripe allows one discount at a time), and the page says so.
      - What the customer agreed to, and when, is written on the Stripe subscription AND in your database (new columns), as your record.
      - Someone who already holds passes is told to change them instead (that page is step 5).
      - Not logged in? The page asks them to log in or sign up, and keeps their choices for when they come back (but never the tick box: that has to be done fresh).
  * THE WELCOME PAGE (/membership/welcome): Stripe sends people back to it after paying. It waits for the payment to be recorded (up to about 18 seconds), then shows "Welcome. Your passes are open."
    with one card per pass and a button into its room.
  * A NEW LISTENER for Stripe's membership messages (/api/membership-webhook), with its own secret, kept apart from the shop's. It records each membership by asking Stripe for the CURRENT state, so a
    late or repeated message cannot undo a cancellation. The shop's own listener now IGNORES membership payments, so a pass can never become a print order.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO YET (and what keeps it safe)
  * It takes NO REAL MONEY: a live Stripe key is refused outright. Real payments wait for the welcome email and the Terms (step 8).
  * Visitors still see "Memberships are not open yet". Only YOU can start a payment (the server checks), until MEMBERSHIP_OPEN is set to yes in Vercel. Do NOT set it yet.
  * No "My passes", change or cancel pages yet (step 5), no locked posts yet (step 6), no emails yet (step 8).

I COULD NOT TRY IT AGAINST STRIPE (my workspace cannot reach it). Every request is tested against a pretend Stripe, so the first real try may show something small; if so, tell me exactly what you see.

 3. Pay with Stripe's test card:  4242 4242 4242 4242,  any future date,  any three digits,  any name and postcode.
 4. You should land on "Welcome. Your passes are open." with a card for each pass. (If it says it is still confirming for more than a minute, step 3 or 4 above was missed.)
 5. LOOK in Supabase (Table Editor, memberships): one row, status active, your passes, consent_at filled in. And in Stripe (test mode): Customers, your subscription, with the discount.
 More to try:  the card 4000 0000 0000 0002 is declined (you stay on Stripe's page);  trying to buy again says you already have passes;  to try again, cancel the subscription in Stripe (the row becomes canceled).
 A PROMO CODE: in Stripe (test mode) make a coupon (say 20% off, repeating, 3 months) and a promotion code WELCOME for it. On the page type WELCOME and press Apply: it should say "Code WELCOME applied: 20% off for 3 months".
 Continue to payment: Stripe should show that discount INSTEAD of the launch offer.
