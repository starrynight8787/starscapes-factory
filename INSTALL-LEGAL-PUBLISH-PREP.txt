GET THE LEGAL PAGES READY TO PUBLISH   starscapes-legal-publish-prep-23oct.zip   (SFW-186, stage 1 of 2)
THIS PACKAGE PUBLISHES NOTHING. The five legal pages stay drafts that only you, signed in as the owner, can see. It prepares the ground so that publishing is one safe command, later, when YOU decide.

WHAT IT DOES
 1. THE PRIVACY POLICY CAN NOW BE PUBLISHED BEFORE THE STAR ATLAS EXISTS. The policy describes the Star Atlas (the stamps, the monthly reading check-in, the optional survey), which is not built yet. A test used to forbid publishing the
    policy until it was, and it was right to: a policy must not describe data the site does not collect. But the policy has to be public at opening (every sign-up form links to it). So the full Atlas wording stays written and tested, and the page
    the site SHOWS leaves it out until the switch STAR_ATLAS_LIVE (src/data/legalPages.js) is set to true on the day the Atlas goes live. What is left out: one section, FOUR rows of the "what we collect" table (the atlas, the monthly reading check-in,
    "the country you choose" which is for the atlas's first country stamp, and the optional survey) and ONE retention bullet. Nothing else changes, and no other page is touched.
    WHAT YOU WILL SEE AS OWNER: the Privacy policy draft now has 14 sections (no "Your Star Atlas and reading check-ins"), 11 rows in its table (not 15), and 10 points under "How long we keep it" (not 11).
 2. ONE COMMAND TO PUBLISH (tools/publish-legal-pages.py), for stage 2. It has a check mode that changes nothing. Publishing turns all five pages on, puts the date on them (today's date, or one you choose), and opens the checkout rail
    (LEGAL_PAGES_PUBLISHED). It writes nothing unless everything is exactly as expected, so it can never leave the site half-published, and it is safe to run twice.
 3. THE TESTS NOW WORK IN BOTH STATES. 14 tests assumed "the pages are drafts" and would have failed on publishing day. They now run only while the pages are drafts (test/legalState.js), and a new set (test/legal-states.test.jsx) runs only once they are
    published: each page opens for a visitor with a real date, a contents list, no draft note and no gap; the footer links a visitor to all five; the pages the checkout points at are ready. A test fails if the pages are ever half-way between the two states.
    I published a COPY and ran all of this: the published state passes too.

WHAT IS NOT IN IT: no publishing, no change a visitor can see (the pages are still drafts), and no change to any other page. The Star Atlas wording is not deleted: it is held back.

TESTS: new files test/star-atlas-switch.test.js (not a word of the atlas shows while it is off: no atlas, stardust, check-in, survey, country stamp or taste week; exactly one section, four rows and one bullet are gone and nothing else; the other four pages are exactly as written;
switching it on gives back the full text; the site reads the switched page, never the full text), test/publish-legal-pages.test.js (the publisher on copies of the real files: check mode writes nothing; publishing changes only the five ready flags, the five dates and the rail, and undoing those gives back the originals;
today's date; safe twice; refuses, and writes nothing, for a bad date, two arguments, a half-way state, an already-open rail, a missing page, or the wrong folder) and test/legal-states.test.jsx. 19 deliberate breakages, ALL caught.
WHOLE SUITE: see the figure in the chat (draft state, and the published state).
