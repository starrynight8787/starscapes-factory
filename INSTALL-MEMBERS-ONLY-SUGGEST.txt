SUGGESTING AND VOTING ARE FOR BOOK CLUB MEMBERS   starscapes-members-only-suggest-21oct.zip   (SFW-206, SFW-249)
(Code, tests, and TWO short steps in Supabase. Released through a preview first.)

YOUR DECISIONS (21 October 2026): suggesting AND voting are for Book Club members only, on BOTH boxes (Book Club: what to read next; Story Ledger: what to audit next). The Story Ledger stays free to watch: everyone can still see what has been suggested and the vote totals. You (the owner) can always suggest and vote.

WHAT CHANGES
 - THE PAGE (src/suggest): a signed-out visitor still gets "Log in to suggest or vote" (its second line now says suggesting and voting are for Book Club members). A signed-in visitor WITHOUT a Book Club pass sees the list and totals, the vote buttons switched off, and a plain box: "Suggesting and voting are for Book Club members. Everyone can see what has been suggested. To suggest something or vote, you need the Book Club pass." with a "Choose your passes" button to the membership page. A Book Club member, or you, sees the form and working vote buttons as before. While the page is still finding out who someone is, it shows neither (so nothing flashes the wrong way). A membership that cannot be read counts as NO pass, never as a member.
 - WHO COUNTS: a Book Club pass that is active, in a trial, or past due while Stripe retries a failed payment (about two weeks), the same rule as every other pass. A cancelled or unpaid pass does not.
 - THE DATABASE (sql/suggestions.sql): the rule for adding a suggestion and for casting a vote now also requires is_book_club_member() (which counts you, the owner). The two old rules that let any signed-in visitor in are replaced (their old names are dropped). Seeing the list and totals is unchanged: still open to everyone. Taking your own vote back is still allowed for anyone.
 - sql/book-details.sql gets the same one-line change as the membership tidy package (a pass that is past due still counts as a member). It is in BOTH packages on purpose: the page's rule and the database's rule must say the same thing, and a test checks it. Whichever package you install first makes the change; the second one says "already done". Nothing breaks either way.
 - The "That did not go through" message now also says that suggesting and voting are for Book Club members.

NOTHING PUBLIC CHANGES TODAY IF THE BOXES ARE STILL SWITCHED OFF (they start off; you switch each on in Admin, Suggestions). Check there before you switch either on.

 1. Open supabase.com, sign in, open your Starscapes Factory project, click "SQL Editor" in the left menu, then "New query".
 2. First a check. Paste this and press Run. It should show one row containing: is_book_club_member
    (if the cell is EMPTY, tell me and do not go on):

 3. Click "New query" again. Paste ALL of this (it makes sure the member check counts a payment being retried, then swaps the two rules) and press Run. It should say "Success. No rows returned":

create or replace function public.is_book_club_member() returns boolean
language sql stable security definer set search_path = public as $$
  select lower(coalesce(auth.jwt() ->> 'email', '')) = 'gillian@gillianbennet.com'
    or exists (select 1 from public.memberships m where m.user_id = auth.uid() and m.status in ('active', 'trialing', 'past_due') and 'book-club' = any (m.passes));
$$;
revoke all on function public.is_book_club_member() from public;
grant execute on function public.is_book_club_member() to authenticated;

drop policy if exists "signed-in people suggest, three a day, while the space is on" on public.suggestions;
drop policy if exists "Book Club members suggest, three a day, while the space is on" on public.suggestions;
create policy "Book Club members suggest, three a day, while the space is on" on public.suggestions for insert to authenticated
  with check (
    public.is_book_club_member()
    and user_id = auth.uid()
    and status = 'pending'
    and exists (select 1 from public.suggestion_spaces s where s.space = suggestions.space and s.enabled)
    and (select count(*) from public.suggestions x where x.user_id = auth.uid() and x.created_at > now() - interval '1 day') < 3
  );

drop policy if exists "vote for an approved suggestion in a space that is on" on public.suggestion_votes;
drop policy if exists "Book Club members vote for an approved suggestion in a space that is on" on public.suggestion_votes;
create policy "Book Club members vote for an approved suggestion in a space that is on" on public.suggestion_votes for insert to authenticated
  with check (
    public.is_book_club_member()
    and user_id = auth.uid()
    and exists (
      select 1 from public.suggestions g join public.suggestion_spaces s on s.space = g.space
      where g.id = suggestion_votes.suggestion_id and g.status in ('approved', 'chosen') and s.enabled
    )
  );
