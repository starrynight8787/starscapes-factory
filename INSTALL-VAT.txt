VAT SHOWN ON ITS OWN LINE AT CHECKOUT   starscapes-vat-checkout-20oct.zip
(NO Supabase step. One thing to check in Stripe afterwards, below. Memberships stay closed to visitors; only you can start a checkout.)

WHAT IT DOES (Gill's decision, 20 October 2026)
 * The price on the site stays the price the member pays: £5 is £5. It INCLUDES VAT. At checkout Stripe now shows the VAT on its own line, as a breakdown of that same price, never added on top.
   At 20 percent: £5 = £4.17 + £0.83 VAT, £7 = £5.83 + £1.17, £10 = £8.33 + £1.67, £12 = £10.00 + £2.00. A year (ten months): £50 = £41.67 + £8.33 ... £120 = £100.00 + £20.00.
   The launch offer (half price) works the same way: VAT is worked out on what is actually paid.
 * The VAT also goes onto the subscription, so every renewal carries it, and changing passes keeps it.
 * A checkout is NEVER started without the VAT rate: if Stripe cannot give it, the member sees "We couldn't start the payment just now" and nothing is taken.
 * The first time anyone starts a checkout in a Stripe mode (test, then live), the site makes ONE tax rate in Stripe named VAT (20 percent, included in the price, tagged as ours) and reuses it after that.
 * The choose-your-passes page says: "Prices include VAT, which is shown separately at checkout. Passes are for customers in the UK for now."
   (UK customers only is the plan until you decide otherwise: VAT on digital services is due where the customer lives, so selling to the EU would mean registering there.)

NOT CHANGED: nothing about what a pass costs; the Terms (the subscription terms come next); the change-of-passes rule (waiting for your answer).
