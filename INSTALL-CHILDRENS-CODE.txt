CHILDREN'S CODE: SARAH JUNIOR'S CLUBHOUSE   starscapes-childrens-code-22oct.zip   (SFW-264)
(Code, wording and tests. No Supabase step. Released through a preview first.)

WHY: Gill confirmed on 22 October 2026 that Sarah Junior's Clubhouse (the Den) is FOR CHILDREN, so the ICO's Children's Code applies to it. The written assessment is attached to SFW-264. The rule that follows:
a child is never asked for anything on its pages.

WHAT CHANGES (what you will SEE)
 1. THE CLUBHOUSE'S PAGES NO LONGER SHOW THE NEWSLETTER BOX. It sits in the footer, which appears on every page, so a child could type an email address on a children's page. It is now hidden on every page under
    /sarah-jrs-den (the front page, the YouTube Series page, and any door). On every other page it is exactly as before.
 2. "OUR PROMISE TO YOU", a small card in plain words, appears on the Clubhouse's front page (under the picture) and at the bottom of the YouTube Series page:
      We don't ask you for your name or your email. / We don't follow you around the internet. / A video only starts when you press play.
    plus a line for the grown-up with a link to the Privacy policy. Every line is true of what the site does, and tests check it.
 3. EVERY EMAIL FORM NOW SAYS IT IS FOR GROWN-UPS: "For grown-ups: you must be 18 or over to sign up. If you are younger, please ask a grown-up to do it for you." (the newsletter box, the free ebook form, and the
    "tell me when it opens" form on the Explorer's Bay taped doors).
 4. THE SIGN UP PAGE says: "Accounts are for people aged 18 or over. If you are younger, please ask a grown-up."
 5. PRIVACY POLICY, SECTION 12 (CHILDREN) IS REWRITTEN (the page is still a draft, only you see it): the shop, accounts and passes are for adults (18 or over); the Clubhouse is for children and what it does and does
    not do; and how a grown-up can ask for a child's information to be deleted. It no longer says only "This shop is for adults".
WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE: nothing is collected from a child; there is still no age check (it would mean collecting MORE from children); the three other Clubhouse doors still show "opening soon".

TESTS: 25 new in test/children-code.test.jsx (which pages count as the Clubhouse, including that a look-alike address does not; no newsletter box on any Clubhouse path and still there everywhere else; no page
component of the Clubhouse can draw or import an email form; the grown-ups sentence on each email form and the sign-up page; the promise card and where it appears; the promise is TRUE: nothing loads from YouTube
until play, the privacy-enhanced player with no related videos, and no page fetches anything straight from YouTube or Google; Privacy section 12). Two existing footer tests were updated to allow exactly one condition on
the newsletter box (the Clubhouse), and to keep forbidding any condition about being signed in or holding a pass. 15 deliberate breakages, ALL caught (the email box coming back on the Clubhouse; a look-alike address
matching; only the front page counting; each grown-ups sentence dropped; the promise dropped or shown in every room; Privacy section 12 reverting; a promise line removed; a YouTube picture loaded directly; an email
form pulled into a Clubhouse page; "related videos" coming back).
WHOLE SUITE on a copy of your newest commit (cec5588) with this installed: 4,032 tests, 0 failing (that includes this installer's own 9 safety tests); build fine; 12 functions; health check 216 of 216.
