WHAT IT BUILDS (from your Figma design "Row: Home v2")
Under the floor plan, which does not change, the home page now has:
  * NEXT EVENT (wide) and MEMBERSHIP (narrow) side by side. Next event shows the soonest event you have added that has not finished: its name, the day and time
    in London time, a line about it, an "Add to calendar" button (a calendar file that works in any calendar app) and a button to YouTube or to your own link.
    When nothing is planned it says "No event planned yet. Check back soon."  Membership says "Into the Nebula: every star starts somewhere." and, honestly,
    that it is not open yet, with a button that cannot be pressed ("Coming soon"). Nothing in that box promises anything that does not exist yet.
  * FEATURED ROOM: one room with its picture, name, tagline, description and a "Step inside" button. It is the Art Loft until you choose another.
  * LATEST POST: your newest published post from any room, with its room and date, "Read the post" and "See all posts".
The old row of three latest posts is gone, as in your sketch.
A NEW TOOL IN ADMIN, "Home page" (/admin/home): choose the featured room, and add, change or delete events.

ALSO IN THIS PACKAGE: THE TAB ICON IS NOW THE BANNER'S MARK, THE STAR WITH ITS THREE TRAILS (your request). It replaces the star-on-its-own icon from the tab icon update
(it showed in your tab as a thin sliver). It is drawn from the banner's own shapes, in the banner's blue, with the trails a little bolder than in the banner so they
still show at 16 pixels, and a navy version for phone home screens. A test fails if the icon ever stops matching the banner's star and all three trails.
Browsers keep old tab icons for a while: after Vercel says Ready, open the site in a private window to see it at once (Safari can take a day or so).
