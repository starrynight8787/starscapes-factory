WHAT CHANGES (all in the Privacy policy)
 1. THE "READING HABITS" SECTION IS REPLACED by "Your Star Atlas and reading check-ins", in the same place (between "Your profile and what other people see" and "Cookies"). It says: what is kept (the stamps, what earned them and when; stardust spent; taste week dates; the country; the number from 0 to 3 and only if you choose a genre; the optional survey); only you can see and change your own atlas; reading habits are only ever looked at as totals, shown only once at least 5 members have checked in that month ("Fewer than 5 so far" until then, the download follows the same rule, and a genre chosen by fewer than 5 is grouped as "Other"); we could look in the database but we don't, and the admin page cannot show one member's answers; the genre is optional and the stardust counts either way; names, email, location and reading answers are never put into AI tools (AI tools only ever see the totals); not shared except with the database provider that stores it; deleted with the account.
    ONE WORDING CHANGE you should know about: the old paragraph said "We never look at one member's answers". The new one says "We could see one member's answers by looking directly in our database, but we don't, and the admin page has no way to show them", which is how the policy already talks about votes, and is the more honest of the two.
 2. FOUR NEW ROWS in "What we collect and why", each with its legal reason: the Star Atlas (contract), the monthly reading check-in (contract for the number, consent for the genre, legitimate interest in seeing totals), the country (contract), and the optional year-one survey (consent).
 3. ONE NEW LINE in "How long we keep it": the Star Atlas, check-ins and survey answers are kept until you ask us to delete your account (the same as your profile).
 It deliberately says NOTHING about the later features (the Explorers Map, cities, leaderboards, comparisons): a test checks that. It is a first draft for the solicitor to check, and not legal advice.
